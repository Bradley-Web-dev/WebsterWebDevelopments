01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WebsterWebDevelopments ("WebsterWebDevelopments," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our website design and development services and this website. It applies to visitors of this website and to clients who engage our services.

02 Information We Collect

This is a static website, and this particular site does not automatically collect visitor data on its own. Information may be collected only when it is voluntarily submitted, such as through the contact form.

03 Information You Provide

When you use the contact form or otherwise reach out to WebsterWebDevelopments, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, and project details. This information is used to respond to your inquiry.

04 How Information Is Used

Information you provide is used to respond to inquiries, discuss and deliver project work, and communicate with you about services you've requested. Information is not sold to third parties.

05 Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies.

06 Analytics

No analytics service is built into this site.

07 Third-Party Services

WebsterWebDevelopments may use third-party tools to operate this website or deliver services (for example, hosting, email, or form-processing providers).

08 Data Retention

Information submitted through the contact form is retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or deliver agreed-upon services, unless a longer retention period is required by law or by an ongoing client relationship.

09 Data Security

WebsterWebDevelopments takes reasonable steps to protect information shared with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10 Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of information you've provided. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

11 Children's Privacy

This website and our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so it can be removed.

12 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
